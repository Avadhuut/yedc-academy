# Business Requirements

**Project Name:** YEDC Academy

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the business requirements for YEDC Academy.

It explains the business problems, objectives, stakeholders, constraints, and expected outcomes.

---

# 2. Business Background

Young Entrepreneur Development Centre (YEDC) currently delivers entrepreneurship training through offline classroom programs.

To increase reach and scale the business, YEDC will launch an online learning platform.

---

# 3. Business Problems

## Limited Reach
Only local students can attend offline training.

## Limited Capacity
Classroom seats restrict growth.

## Repetitive Teaching
The same sessions are delivered repeatedly.

## Time Constraints
Working professionals cannot attend fixed schedules.

## Limited Scalability
Growth depends on more classrooms and trainers.

---

# 4. Proposed Solution

Provide an online platform where users can:

- Register
- Browse courses
- Purchase courses
- Watch recorded lessons
- Download resources
- Track learning progress

---

# 5. Business Objectives

## Short Term
- Digitize existing courses
- Reach learners across India
- Generate online revenue

## Medium Term
- Launch multiple entrepreneurship courses
- Build a student community

## Long Term
- Become India's leading entrepreneurship academy
- Offer consulting
- Launch certification programs

---

# 6. Stakeholders

Primary stakeholders:

- Students
- YEDC Management
- Trainers
- Administrators

---

# 7. Business Goals

- Increase student reach
- Reduce operational cost
- Improve learning accessibility
- Increase revenue
- Strengthen the YEDC brand

---

# 8. Success Metrics

- Registered users
- Paid enrollments
- Revenue
- Course completion rate
- Student satisfaction
- Returning customers

---

# 9. Business Scope

## MVP

- Public website
- Authentication
- Course catalog
- Student dashboard
- Video learning
- Progress tracking
- Admin dashboard
- Mock payment

## Future

- Live classes
- AI mentor
- Mobile apps
- Community
- Certifications
- Consulting

---

# 10. Business Assumptions

- Learners have internet access.
- Users are willing to buy online courses.
- Trainers can create quality video content.

---

# 11. Business Constraints

- Limited initial budget
- Small development team
- Prefer free/open-source tools during MVP

---

# 12. Risks

- Low course sales
- Strong competition
- Poor user engagement

Mitigation:
- Launch MVP quickly
- Gather feedback
- Improve continuously

---

# 13. Expected Benefits

- Nationwide reach
- Scalable revenue
- Digital brand presence
- Reusable learning content
