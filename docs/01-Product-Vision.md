# Product Vision

**Project Name:** YEDC Academy

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision, mission, and direction for YEDC Academy.

It explains why the platform exists, what problem it solves, who it serves, and what success looks like.

---

# 2. Vision Statement

> To become India's most trusted platform for entrepreneurship education and business growth.

---

# 3. Mission Statement

Make practical entrepreneurship education accessible, affordable, and available to anyone through structured online courses created by experienced business mentors.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

Current YEDC training is primarily offline.

Challenges include:
- Limited classroom capacity
- Limited geographic reach
- Repetitive teaching
- Limited scalability
- Working professionals cannot always attend

---

# 5. Proposed Solution

Build an online learning platform where learners can:

- Register
- Browse courses
- Purchase courses
- Watch recorded lessons
- Download learning resources
- Track progress
- Learn anytime from anywhere

---

# 6. Product Vision

YEDC Academy is a complete entrepreneurship learning platform, not just a course-selling website.

The platform aims to help people:

- Start businesses
- Grow businesses
- Learn practical entrepreneurship
- Access expert guidance
- Build long-term business success

---

# 7. Target Audience

## MVP Audience

- Aspiring entrepreneurs starting their first business

## Future Audience

- Existing business owners
- MSMEs
- Students
- Working professionals
- Women entrepreneurs
- Startup founders

---

# 8. Business Model

Initial Model:
- Individual recorded courses
- One-time payment
- Lifetime access

Future:
- Memberships
- Live workshops
- Consulting
- Certifications
- Business templates

---

# 9. Value Proposition

Learn practical entrepreneurship from experienced mentors without attending offline classes.

---

# 10. Success Statement

> "This is the best place in India to learn how to start and grow a business."

---

# 11. Success Metrics

- Registered users
- Paid students
- Revenue
- Course completion rate
- Student satisfaction
- Monthly active users

---

# 12. MVP Scope

- Public website
- Authentication
- Course catalog
- Student dashboard
- Video learning
- Progress tracking
- Admin dashboard
- Mock payment

---

# 13. Guiding Principles

- Build MVP first
- Solve business problems
- Design before coding
- Build feature-by-feature
- Maintain documentation
- Focus on user experience
